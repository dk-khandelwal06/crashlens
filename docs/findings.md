# CrashLens Empirical Findings & Analytical Insights

## 1. Executive Summary

Analysis of 26,030 validated personal injury collisions recorded across Greater London in 2022 revealed striking spatial and temporal concentrations:
- **Spatial Concentration:** 64.69% of all collisions occurred within 432 dense geographical hotspots representing a small fraction of the capital's total road network.
- **Background Noise:** 35.31% of collisions were solitary, non-clustered incidents.
- **Top Hotspot (Zone #6):** Located in Central London, this zone exhibited 6,745 collision records, 26 fatalities, 1,232 serious injuries, and a severity-weighted risk score of 10,675.

## 2. Diurnal Temporal Peaks

- **Evening Rush Peak:** 18:00 emerged as the single highest collision hour across the 24-hour cycle.
- **Morning Commute Elevated Period:** 07:00–09:00 showed a secondary surge, reflecting multimodal commuter congestion.
- **Nighttime Vulnerability:** While total crash volume declines after 21:00, severe and fatal outcomes represent a higher percentage of nighttime collisions, correlating with reduced visibility and higher vehicle speeds.

## 3. Infrastructure & Environmental Observations

- **Carriageway Distribution:** Single carriageway roadways accounted for the overwhelming majority of collisions, followed by dual carriageways and roundabouts.
- **Weather Breakdown:** 80%+ of recorded incidents occurred under clear/fine weather conditions, demonstrating that driver exposure volume during fair conditions dominates raw collision counts over adverse weather events.
- **Non-Causation Disclosure:** All identified metrics represent historical empirical correlations. They reflect travel exposure and density rather than proven individual causation.
